Resident User Guide

How to check available shifts, book a date, and cancel a booking.

← Back to booking page 日本語

Check available shifts

Open the booking homepage and enter your room number. The calendar will show dates available. The page also lists booked residents by date, including their names and room numbers.

Book a shift

  1. Enter your room number and name.
  2. Select an available date on the calendar.
  3. Select Book this date.
!

Important: Save your cancellation code

Your four-digit code appears after booking and cannot be retrieved later. You need it together with your room number to cancel. Keep it private.

Each room can have one active booking.

Cancel a booking

  1. In Cancel a booking, enter your room number and cancellation code.
  2. Select Cancel my booking and confirm.

New bookings use a four-digit code. You can make up to 10 cancellation attempts per room in each 24-hour period. If you reach the limit, try again after 24 hours.

If you forget your cancel code, please contact the RA.
