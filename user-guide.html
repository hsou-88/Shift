CLEANING SHIFT BOOKING

Resident User Guide

How to check available shifts, book a date, and cancel a booking.

← Back to booking page 日本語

Check available shifts

Open the booking homepage and enter your room number. The calendar will show dates available to your resident group. The page also lists booked residents by date, including their names and room numbers.

Book a shift

  1. Enter your room number and name.
  2. Select an available date on the calendar.
  3. Select Book this date.
  4. Save the four-digit cancellation code shown in the confirmation message. Keep it private. You will need it with your room number to cancel.

Each room can have one active booking. Available dates and spaces depend on the resident group settings made by the administrator.

Cancel a booking

  1. In Cancel a booking, enter your room number and cancellation code.
  2. Select Cancel my booking and confirm.

New bookings use a four-digit code. Earlier 64-character codes are still accepted. You can make up to 10 cancellation attempts per room in each 24-hour period. If you reach the limit, try again after 24 hours.

Bookings created before cancellation codes were introduced cannot be cancelled with a code. Please contact the administrator.
